file-icons-data.js
  Generated by tools/orca-ikonlar.mjs from two sources, nothing typed by hand:
  1. Orca's file icon tables: FILE_ICON_BY_NAME, FILE_ICON_BY_EXTENSION and COMPOUND_EXTENSIONS of
     src/renderer/src/lib/file-icons/file-type-icon-name-table.ts and file-type-icon-extension-table.ts,
     github.com/stablyai/orca, tag v1.4.259 (identical in v1.4.255, where the files sat in src/renderer/src/lib/).
     MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 Lovecast Inc.: see ORCA-LICENSE.txt.
     file-icons.js re-implements the rule order of getFileTypeIcon (file-type-icons.ts) and of FolderIcon
     (src/renderer/src/components/FileTypeIcon.tsx) of the same tag: a modification of that work, under the same license.
  2. The shapes of the Lucide icons those tables name, from the icons/*.svg of the npm package lucide-static 0.577.0
     (the version Orca's lucide-react ^0.577.0 resolves to in its pnpm-lock.yaml); only the child elements of each
     <svg> are kept. ISC License (portions MIT, from Feather): see LUCIDE-LICENSE.txt (the package's LICENSE).
